What we store

Sardinia Skipper has no player accounts and no game servers. Your progress (money, knots, boats, missions, story, settings) is saved only on your own phone. We never receive it.

How to delete your game data

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Sardinia Skipper → Storage and tap Clear storage, or
  2. Uninstall Sardinia Skipper.

This deletes all game data immediately and permanently. Nothing is kept by us.

Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Ads are delivered by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. You can:

  • change your ad consent in the game: Settings → Ad privacy settings;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID: phone Settings → Google → Ads.

Google keeps ad data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Request deletion by email

If you have emailed us (for example for support) and want those emails and your address deleted, send a message to minjauwleonard@gmail.com with the subject "Sardinia Skipper data deletion". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply. Purchase records that Google Play keeps for tax and accounting are handled by Google.
