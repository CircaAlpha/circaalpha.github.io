1. Controller

The controller of your personal data for Sardinia Skipper is CircaAlpha, an independent game developer based in Belgium. Contact: minjauwleonard@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • We have no player accounts and run no game servers. Your progress stays on your device.
  • If the Game shows ads, Google AdMob processes device data to deliver them. Personalised ads are only shown if you consent.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play / Apple. We only learn that a product was bought, never your payment details.

3. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress on your device (money and knots balances, owned boats and items, missions, story progress, settings). This data is not sent to us. You can delete it by uninstalling the Game or clearing its data.

Reminders. If you allow notifications, the Game schedules reminders (for example "your daily gift is ready") locally on your device. No data is sent to us for this, and you can switch reminders off in Settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

When ads are enabled, Google AdMob may collect and process: your device's advertising identifier (AAID / IDFA), IP address, device and operating-system information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction data (e.g. whether an ad was shown or watched).

  • Personalised ads are shown only on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), collected through Google's consent screen when you first start the Game. On iOS you are also asked for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency).
  • If you refuse, Google may still show non-personalised ads and process limited data for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement, based on legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google acts as an independent controller for some of this processing. See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store, who act as independent controllers of your payment data. We receive a confirmation that a product was purchased (product id, order/transaction id) so we can deliver it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of a contract).

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you (legitimate interest / contract), and delete them when no longer needed, at the latest 2 years after our last contact unless we must keep them longer by law (e.g. accounting for refunds).

7. Who else processes data

  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC: Google AdMob (advertising, consent management) and Google Play Billing (purchases).
  • Apple Distribution International: App Store purchases, if you play on iOS.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with anyone else. We have no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of our own in the Game.

7b. International transfers

Google and Apple may process data outside the EEA, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided data, contact us and we will help delete it.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing). Because we hold almost no data about you, most requests concerning ads are best handled through the ad privacy settings or directly with Google. Contact us at minjauwleonard@gmail.com for any request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), Drukpersstraat 35, 1000 Brussels, www.dataprotectionauthority.be, or with the authority in your own country.

10. Security

Your save data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. We do not transmit it.

11. Changes

We will update this policy when the Game changes (for example if we add analytics, crash reporting or cloud saves) and show the new effective date above. Important changes will be announced in the Game.
